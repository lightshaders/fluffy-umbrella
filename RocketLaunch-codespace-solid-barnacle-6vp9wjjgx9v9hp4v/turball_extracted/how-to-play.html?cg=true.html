THE TURBALL BEGINNER GUIDE

How to play Turball

Turball puts you behind the wheel: drive into the ball, aim for the other team's goal, and get back in position before the next shot. Turball runs in your browser, with bot matches, free play and car customization.

Start with free play, then try a match

  1. Open Play → Training and choose free play. Get comfortable driving, braking and turning before chasing the ball at full speed.
  2. Approach the ball from behind it, with your car facing the target. Make a gentle touch, then try adding boost on your next approach.
  3. Open Play → Casual when you are ready for a bot match. Duel is a useful place to practise because each missed touch leaves you responsible for defending your own goal.

Three habits for better shots and saves

  • Line up before accelerating. A controlled approach gives you more time to choose where the ball goes. Full boost is useful after you have a line to the target.
  • Recover toward your goal. After missing a shot, turn back into a defensive position instead of circling underneath the ball. Try to keep yourself between the ball and your goal.
  • Practise one touch at a time. In free play, repeat a ground shot until you can direct it reliably. Then practise jumping into a low ball before attempting higher aerials.

Choose what to practise next

Play → Training includes free play and drills for shooting, aerials and goalkeeping. Play → Competitive lets you challenge ranked bots and build your local career. Current matches are against bots; this guide does not describe live online multiplayer.

Visit the Garage to change your car's look, or explore the Item Shop and Turball Pass for cosmetic rewards.

Do I need to install the game?

No game installation is needed. Open Turball in a browser; the game loads the assets it needs. JavaScript must be enabled to play.
