THE TURBALL PRACTICE GUIDE

Free play & training

A short practice session works best with one clear target: cleaner touches, faster recoveries, or a save you can repeat.

Start in free play

Open Play → Training, choose Free Play, select an arena and confirm. You can work at your own pace without opponents. In Settings → Training, choose Unlimited boost for repeated attempts or Standard boost to practise using the pads around the field.

Disable Restart on Goal keeps play moving after a goal. Leave it off when you want each finish to reset the setup. Show Car Hitbox displays the collision shape if you want to understand where a touch comes from.

Bring the ball to the exercise

Free play has four ball-control shortcuts. These are the default keyboard inputs; check Controls if you have rebound them.

KeyAction
1Take Possession
2Start Dribble
3Pass Ball
4Launch Ball
BackspaceReset Shot

Use possession for controlled first touches, a pass for meeting a moving ball, and a launch for reading its flight. Repeat the same action before adding speed; changing both your approach and the ball setup makes it harder to tell what helped.

Choose a focused drill

  • Striker: practise finishing the supplied setups. Get your car behind the ball before accelerating into it.
  • Goalie: react to an incoming ball and make the save. Recover toward the goal after a touch so you are ready for the next attempt.
  • Aerial: meet an elevated ball. First aim for contact, then work on directing it while landing under control.

Each drill runs through ten attempts and shows your progress. Custom Training offers these same focused categories from one screen.

A simple ten-minute routine

  1. Spend two minutes driving and landing cleanly, using short boosts instead of holding boost continuously.
  2. Spend four minutes on one kind of touch. Note where the ball goes when you strike its middle versus its side.
  3. Finish with a drill, then try the skill in a casual match. Pick the next practice target from a situation you actually found difficult.

When an attempt goes wrong, reset and repeat it with one adjustment. A predictable touch is a stronger foundation than a spectacular shot you cannot reproduce.
