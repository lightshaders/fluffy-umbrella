THE TURBALL COLLECTION GUIDE

Garage & rewards

Build a car you can recognize at a glance, then save the setup so you can come back to it after experimenting.

Put together a loadout

Open the Garage and choose Customize Car. The category tabs cover bodies, decals, paint finish, wheels, boost, goal explosions and engine audio. Select an item to preview it and equip it. Search and rarity or paint filters help narrow a larger collection.

Paint Finish has separate Blue Team and Orange Team colors, with primary and accent choices for each. Check both team previews: a decal can read differently against an orange primary color than a blue one. Goal explosions have their own goal preview so you can see the effect's shape and scale.

Save combinations you like

Use presets in the Garage to keep several loadouts. Give each a recognizable name before trying a new body, paint combination or wheel style. Selecting a saved preset brings its choices back together.

Open the daily crate

The Item Shop includes a Daily Crate with one free opening per UTC day. When it is available, open the Daily Crate tab and choose Open Free Crate. Once claimed, the shop shows the countdown to the next reset at midnight UTC; it is not a separate 24-hour timer from the moment you opened it.

The revealed cosmetic joins Collection. Drops use rarity weights, so a rare-looking item in a preview is not a guarantee that your next crate contains it. If you already have every item in the selected rarity, you can receive a duplicate.

Earn more crates

The Crates tab includes missions for completed matches, wins and team goals. Each card shows its target and progress. Complete a mission to open its crate; owned crates can also come from season-pass rewards. Opening one of these does not consume the daily opening.

Keep your collection

Your local career, collection and loadouts are saved in this browser's site storage. Clearing site data or moving to a different browser can remove access to that local progress. Signing in establishes your account identity; it does not by itself transfer this local collection between devices. Keep using the same browser profile if you want to continue the same career.
