THE TURBALL CONTROL GUIDE

Controls & camera

Set up a layout you can use without looking down. Change one thing at a time, then take a short drive in free play before adjusting anything else.

Know the default inputs

On keyboard, W and S drive forward and reverse; A and D steer. Left mouse boosts, right mouse jumps, and Space switches ball camera. Shift is powerslide on the ground and air roll in the air. Q and E provide dedicated left and right air roll. Press P to pause or resume; Esc also works.

Ball camera keeps the ball in view. Car camera is useful when lining up your route or checking where your wheels will land. Try both at low speed before relying on a quick camera switch during a save.

Make the bindings yours

  1. Open Settings, then Controls. Keyboard and mouse bindings are listed alongside controller bindings.
  2. Select the input you want to change, then press the key, mouse button or controller input you prefer. The small remove button clears a binding; an empty slot adds one.
  3. If you started capturing an input by mistake, press Escape to cancel. Test driving, jump, boost and camera together before beginning a match.

For a controller, check the selected device in Controls, especially if several are connected. The Stick Roles section controls analog steering and aerial movement. Touch players can open Touch Controls to adjust the on-screen layout; keep important buttons comfortably within thumb reach.

Frame the field

In Camera settings, Distance moves the camera closer or farther away, Height raises or lowers it, and Angle changes its tilt. Field of View changes how much of the scene fits on screen. Start with the defaults and adjust in small steps: seeing more field is useful only if you can still judge the ball's distance.

Stiffness changes the camera's response to car movement. Swivel Speed and Transition Speed affect looking around and camera changes. Turn Camera Shake off if impacts make it harder to track the ball. These preferences are saved for the next session.

A quick setup check

Drive a figure eight, powerslide around the ball, jump, boost briefly in the air, and land on your wheels. If a finger has to leave steering to reach a frequent action, revisit that binding. Comfort and a consistent view matter more than copying someone else's settings.
